Common questions

Is dyed film the same as ceramic film in Phoenix heat?

No. Public installer pages treat dyed film as a darker look with weaker infrared rejection that can fade in Arizona sun. Ceramic and spectrally selective films reject more heat at a lighter exterior look. Ask which chemistry is on the bid.

Will a Phoenix HOA allow any window film?

Many Valley HOAs restrict mirror-finish or highly reflective film. Ask for VLT and exterior reflectance on a manufacturer sheet. This site is not legal advice and does not speak for any HOA.

Does a darker tint always block more heat?

No. Darkness is visible light. Heat rejection is a different number. A light ceramic film can reject more infrared than a dark dyed film.

Can film void a dual-pane warranty?

Ask the installer whether the named film is rated for your existing insulated glass. This site does not warrant glass or film.

Should I pick the cheapest square-foot number first?

Public threads compare cheap dyed or unknown film that faded or looked like a mirror from the street. Ask for the film name and spec sheet in writing first.
